Privacy Policy for Prime Shot

Effective Date: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Face Data and Biometric Information

Prime Shot collects and processes face data from the photos you upload to create personalized AI-generated images. This includes:

  • Facial features, expressions, and characteristics from your uploaded photos
  • Facial landmarks and biometric identifiers necessary for AI model training
  • Physical appearance data to enable accurate personalized image generation

Photos and Images

  • Training photos you upload to create your AI model
  • Generated images created by our AI service
  • Metadata associated with your photos (file size, format, creation date)

Usage Information

  • App usage statistics and performance data
  • Generation requests and prompts
  • In-app purchase history

Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crash reports
  • Anonymous analytics data

2. How We Use Your Information

Face Data Processing

Reasons for Processing Face Data:

  • To train a personalized AI model that can generate images featuring your likeness
  • To ensure generated images accurately reflect your facial features and characteristics
  • To provide the core functionality of personalized AI image generation
  • To maintain consistency across multiple generated images

Photo Processing

  • Your uploaded photos are used solely to train your personalized AI model
  • Generated images are created based on your prompts and training data
  • We do not use your photos or face data to train our general AI models or for any other purpose

3. Data Storage and Retention

Face Data Storage Duration

Storage Timeline:

  • Face data is stored for a maximum of 90 days after your last app usage
  • If you remain active, face data is retained to maintain your personalized model
  • Upon account deletion, all face data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Generated images can be stored longer at your discretion but contain no extractable biometric data

Reasons for Storage Duration:

  • 90-day retention allows for model optimization and consistent results
  • Temporary storage enables faster generation times for returning users
  • Extended storage is unnecessary as models can be retrained if needed
  • Automatic deletion protects user privacy while maintaining service quality

Security Measures

  • All face data and photos are encrypted during upload, processing, and storage
  • Face data is processed in secure, isolated environments
  • Access to face data is strictly limited to automated AI training processes
  • No human reviewers have access to your face data or photos

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Access

Face Data Sharing

Third Parties We Share Face Data With:

1. Replicate (AI Processing Service)

  • Reason for Sharing: To process face data and generate personalized images
  • Data Storage: Replicate does NOT store face data - processing is ephemeral
  • Storage Duration: Face data is processed and immediately deleted (< 24 hours)
  • Why This Duration: Required only for active image generation processes

2. Google Cloud Storage (Secure Storage)

  • Reason for Sharing: Temporary encrypted storage during processing
  • Data Storage: Google Cloud stores encrypted face data temporarily
  • Storage Duration: Maximum 90 days, automatically deleted afterward
  • Why This Duration: Enables model persistence and faster processing

Third-Party Privacy Practices

Replicate's Face Data Practices:

  • Face data is processed in memory only and not stored permanently
  • Data is deleted immediately after processing completion
  • Replicate does not use face data for their own model training
  • Processing occurs in secure, isolated computing environments

Google Cloud's Face Data Practices:

  • Face data is stored encrypted and isolated in dedicated storage buckets
  • Google does not access or use face data for their own services
  • Data retention follows our specified 90-day maximum policy
  • Automatic deletion ensures compliance with our privacy commitments

What We DON'T Share

  • We never sell, rent, or share your face data with advertisers
  • Face data is not used for marketing, advertising, or research purposes
  • Your face data is not shared with social media platforms or data brokers
  • No unauthorized third parties have access to your biometric information

5. Your Rights and Controls

Face Data Controls

  • Access: View what face data we have collected about you
  • Deletion: Delete all face data and training photos immediately
  • Portability: Export your generated images (which contain no extractable biometric data)
  • Opt-out: Stop all face data processing by deleting your account

6. Children's Privacy

Prime Shot is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect face data or personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

7. International Users

Your face data may be processed in the United States where our service providers (Replicate, Google Cloud) operate. We ensure all international transfers comply with applicable privacy laws and maintain the same level of protection described in this policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes affecting face data collection or storage will be communicated through the app with explicit consent required before continuing to use face data features.
