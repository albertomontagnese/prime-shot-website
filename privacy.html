🔒 Face Data Collection & Usage

This section addresses Apple App Store requirements for face data handling:

What face data does Prime Shot collect?

Prime Shot processes facial features, expressions, physical characteristics, and facial geometry from your uploaded photos. This includes but is not limited to: facial landmarks, skin tone, eye color, hair characteristics, facial structure, and expressions captured in your uploaded images.

How is face data used?

Primary Use: Face data is used exclusively to create your personalized AI model for generating photos based on your prompts. This includes training the model to recognize your features and generate realistic images that maintain your identity.

Quality Improvement: We analyze generation quality to improve our AI algorithms, but this is done using aggregated, anonymized data only.

Service Delivery: Face data enables the core functionality of creating personalized AI-generated photos that look like you.

Is face data shared with third parties?

No Commercial Sharing: We never sell, rent, or commercially share your face data with any third parties.

Service Providers: Face data is processed by our AI service provider (Replicate API) under strict data processing agreements that prohibit any use beyond providing our service.

Storage Location: Face data is securely stored on encrypted Google Cloud Storage servers in the United States, with additional processing occurring on Replicate's secure infrastructure.

How long is face data retained?

Active Use: Face data is retained for as long as you actively use Prime Shot and maintain your account.

Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all face data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

User Control: You can request immediate deletion of your face data at any time through app settings or by contacting privacy@primeshot.app.

1. Information We Collect

Photos and Images

  • Training photos you upload to create your AI model
  • Generated images created by our AI service
  • Metadata associated with your photos (file size, format, upload timestamp)
  • Facial features and characteristics extracted for AI model training

Usage Information

  • App usage statistics and performance data
  • Generation requests and prompts you submit
  • In-app purchase history and billing information
  • Feature usage patterns and preferences

Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crash reports
  • Anonymous analytics data for app improvement
  • Network connectivity information

2. How We Use Your Information

Photo Processing

  • Your uploaded photos are used solely to train your personalized AI model
  • Generated images are created based on your prompts and training data
  • We do not use your photos to train our general AI models or for any other users
  • Face data is processed to ensure generated images maintain your identity and characteristics

Service Improvement

  • Analytics help us improve app performance and user experience
  • Crash reports help us fix bugs and stability issues
  • Aggregated usage data helps us develop new features

Communication

  • Sending you notifications about generation completion
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Important updates about service changes or security issues

3. Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

  • All photos are encrypted during upload, processing, and storage using AES-256 encryption
  • We use industry-standard security protocols and regular security audits
  • Access to your data is strictly limited to essential operations and authorized personnel
  • Multi-factor authentication protects administrative access

Data Retention

  • Training photos are stored securely for as long as you use the service
  • Generated images are stored in your account until you delete them
  • Face data and AI models are retained until account deletion
  • You can delete your data at any time through the app settings

Data Location

  • Primary data storage: Google Cloud Storage (United States)
  • AI processing: Replicate infrastructure (United States)
  • All data remains within secure, SOC 2 compliant facilities

4. Data Sharing

Third-Party Services

  • Google Cloud Storage: Secure photo storage with enterprise-grade encryption
  • Replicate API: AI image generation under strict data processing agreements
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Payment processing and app distribution
  • All third-party services are bound by data processing agreements that prohibit unauthorized data use

No Selling of Data

  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal photos with third parties for commercial purposes
  • We do not use your photos for advertising or marketing to other users
  • Your generated images remain private to your account
  • Face data is never shared outside of essential service operations

Legal Requirements

  • We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request
  • We will notify users of legal requests unless prohibited by law
  • We challenge overly broad or inappropriate legal requests

5. Your Rights

Access and Control

  • View and download all your generated images through the app
  • Delete your training photos and generated content at any time
  • Request complete account deletion and data removal
  • Access your data processing history and generation logs

Data Portability

  • Download your data in machine-readable formats upon request
  • Transfer your data to other services (where technically feasible)

Communication Preferences

  • Control notification settings for generation completion
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Maintain essential service communications for security and billing

6. Children's Privacy

Prime Shot is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@primeshot.app.

7. International Users

Prime Shot is operated from the United States. Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using Prime Shot, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States.

European Users: If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability, the right to rectification, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

California Users: California residents have additional rights under CCPA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to delete personal information.
