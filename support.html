Support & Help

Get help with Prime Shot and find answers to frequently asked questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Prime Shot work?
Prime Shot uses advanced AI technology to create personalized images. You upload 10-20 photos of yourself, and our AI learns your unique characteristics to generate new images based on your text prompts.
What kind of photos should I upload?
Upload clear, well-lit photos that show your face clearly. Include different angles, expressions, and lighting conditions for best results. Avoid sunglasses, heavy makeup, or filters that might obscure your natural features.
How long does image generation take?
Image generation typically takes 30-60 seconds per batch of 4 images. The initial AI model training may take a few minutes after you upload your photos.
Is my data safe and private?
Yes, your privacy is our top priority. All photos and face data are encrypted and securely processed. We never sell or share your personal information. Face data is automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I delete my photos and data?
Absolutely. You can delete your training photos and all associated data at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account, all face data is permanently removed within 30 days.
What image styles can I create?
You can create a wide variety of images including professional headshots, casual photos, artistic portraits, themed images (like "professional in a suit" or "casual at the beach"), and creative scenarios. Simply describe what you want in your text prompt.
How many images can I generate?
Prime Shot includes 1 free generation (4 images). After that, you can purchase generation packs:
  • Starter Pack: 5 generations (20 images) - $3.99
  • Pro Pack: 15 generations (60 images) - $9.99
  • Ultimate Pack: 200 generations (800 images) - $99.99
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled through the App Store. You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase by contacting Apple Support or through your App Store purchase history.
The app crashes or won't load. What should I do?
Try these troubleshooting steps:
  • Force close and restart the app
  • Restart your device
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Update to the latest version of Prime Shot
  • If problems persist, contact our support team
My generated images don't look like me. What's wrong?
This can happen if:
  • You didn't upload enough photos (upload 10-20 for best results)
  • Your photos were low quality or unclear
  • Photos showed very different looks (different hair, makeup, etc.)
Try uploading more consistent, high-quality photos and retrain your model.

Troubleshooting

Photo Upload Issues
If photos won't upload:
  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure photos are in JPEG or PNG format
  • Try uploading fewer photos at once
  • Restart the app and try again
Purchase Problems
If in-app purchases aren't working:
  • Ensure you're signed into the App Store
  • Check your payment method is valid
  • Try restarting the app
  • Contact Apple Support for payment issues
