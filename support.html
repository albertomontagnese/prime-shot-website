Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Prime Shot

How does Prime Shot work?

Prime Shot uses advanced AI technology to create professional-quality photos from your uploaded images. Simply upload 10+ photos of yourself, describe the type of photo you want, and our AI will generate 4 unique variations based on your description.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. Your photos are encrypted during upload and storage, never shared with third parties, and you can delete your data at any time. We use enterprise-grade security to protect your information.

What kind of photos should I upload?

For best results, upload 10-20 clear photos of yourself from different angles, with good lighting, and showing your face clearly. Include both close-up and full-body shots, and avoid group photos or images with sunglasses.

How long does it take to generate photos?

Photo generation typically takes 5-15 minutes depending on server load. You'll receive a notification when your photos are ready to download.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are processed through the App Store or Google Play. Refund requests must be submitted through your respective app store. We're committed to your satisfaction, so please contact us if you're not happy with your results.

What face data does Prime Shot collect?

Face Data Collection: Prime Shot processes facial features, expressions, and physical characteristics from your uploaded photos to create your personalized AI model.

Usage: This data is used solely to generate your requested photos and improve generation quality. We do not use your face data for any other purposes.

Storage & Retention: Face data is securely stored on encrypted servers and retained for as long as you use the service. You can delete your data at any time through app settings.

Sharing: We never share your face data with third parties. Data is processed by our secure AI service providers (Replicate API) under strict privacy agreements.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app, or by contacting our support team. This will permanently remove all your photos, generated images, and face data from our servers.
